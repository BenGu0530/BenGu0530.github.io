Allometric Scaling Laws for Bipedal Robots
Dec 2023 – Sep 2026
Student Researcher, Robomechanics Lab · PI: Prof. Aaron Johnson
  • How should a biped's design change with its size? A survey of existing bipedal robots shows mass scales as L2 rather than the isometric L3.
  • Mugatu (0.15 m legs) and Zippy (0.025 m legs) simulated in Drake across a 60× range of leg lengths: walking speed follows dynamic similarity (v ∝ L½), joint torque scales as mL, and feasible foot size scales linearly with leg length.
